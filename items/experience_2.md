Experience 2 Reflection: Retail Sales Associate Role

What was it that you did?
During the summer, I worked as a retail sales associate at a clothing store located in the mall. My main duties included folding clothes, managing the fitting rooms, and handling various other store tasks.

When did you do it?
I started working there in July and am still currently employed there. However, my hours have decreased recently due to starting school and moving.

What was good about it?
I really enjoyed the workplace atmosphere because it felt very professional, and my coworkers were really kind. The pay was reliable on a bi-weekly schedule. Having a job over the summer gave me something productive to do without feeling overly demanding, even though the store could get quite busy on weekends.

What was your favourite memory of it?
My favorite memory was getting to hang out with my best friend, who worked in the mall's food court. We would meet up during breaks, before my shift started, or whenever I wrapped up early. Spending that time together was extra meaningful because she moved away to Europe for the school year, so I really cherish the moments we spent together while she was still here.

What didn't you enjoy about it?
There wasn't much I disliked, but working closing shifts until 11:00 PM could get pretty exhausting. Additionally, commuting took 20 minutes or longer, so I always had to plan around traffic to ensure I wasn't late.

Overall Grade
A – couldn't be better
