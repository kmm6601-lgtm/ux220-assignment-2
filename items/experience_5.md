Experience 5: Learning Guitar

What did you do?
I tried learning guitar over the summer after my cousin gave me one of her old guitars. I had always wanted to learn, but I never got around to it because it seemed expensive and time-consuming.

When did you do it?
I did it over the summer.

What did you enjoy about it?
I enjoyed learning to play because it was a calming distraction, and I liked learning songs on the guitar.

What was your favourite memory of it?
My favourite memory was learning a song called “Best Part.” It is a very easy song to learn, and I love it.

What didn't you enjoy about it?
I didn’t enjoy how long it takes to become proficient. I become easily distracted, so if I am not immediately good at something, I tend to give up. However, because it was an old guitar, one of the strings snapped unexpectedly, and I stopped playing because I was too lazy to replace it. Perhaps I will return to it and try one day again.

Overall Grade
C. It was ok
