Experience 1 Reflection: Boba Shop Role

What was it that you did?
For my very first job, I worked at a boba shop where I handled virtually every position. My duties included serving food to tables, washing dishes, preparing food and beverages, operating the cash register, managing inventory and stocking, and assisting in the Asian makeup and beauty department, among other tasks.

When did you do it?
I began working there in March of last year, right in the middle of a heavy workload of final assignments, projects, and exams, making it an incredibly stressful period.

What was good about it?
The position gave me valuable hands-on experience and equipped me with a broad range of skills that I can carry forward into future jobs.

What was your favourite memory of it?
Closing the store alongside my coworkers was my fondest memory. Even though closing shifts were demanding and frequently required staying overtime—which I disliked—it gave us a chance to relax and really get to know each other.

What didn't you enjoy about it?
My overall experience was negatively impacted by my boss, who was highly unprofessional and unkind. They withheld paychecks and engaged in other labor law violations, which ultimately prompted me to resign.

Overall Grade
F - I still have nightmares about it
