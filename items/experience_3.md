Experience 3 Reflection: Finding a House

What did you do?
Finding off-campus housing for my second year required steady effort and a significant time commitment. I toured numerous properties and created an Excel spreadsheet to track budgets, locations, pros and cons, and potential cost savings compared to living in campus residence.

When did you do it?
I began searching for student housing last November. I reached out to several landlords via phone and email to schedule in-person viewings before signing any lease, ensuring the properties met my standards.

What were the positive aspects?
The process was encouraging because finding a place would help relieve the financial strain of on-campus housing, which is quite costly for what it offers.

What was your favourite memory?
My favourite memory was touring houses with my first-year roommate. We still live together in our second year and found a fantastic house that suits both of our needs, alongside a great group of housemates.

What challenges did you face?
The search felt overwhelming at times due to the weight of the decision. Securing suitable roommates and housing is stressful, especially when committing to a 12-month lease for a long-term living environment.

Overall Grade
A – Couldn't be better
