Experience 4: School Teacher Assistant

What did you do?
I completed my co-op as a student teacher assistant. I taught Grade Ones. I prepared lesson plans, marked their work, taught short lessons, and supervised them during lunch, recess, field trips, and more.

When did you do it?
I did it in my senior year of high school. I'm not sure why I chose that as my co-op. I think it was more convenient for travel, as it was an elementary school close to my high school. I never really had an interest in youth and children's studies or in being a teacher.

What was good about it?
It was good because it was a required course for my technology Red Seal, and I’d say it was an easy credit.

What was your favourite memory of it?
My favourite memory was being someone the kids could look up to. They trusted me and turned to me for help, so it felt great to be a role model.

What didn't you enjoy about it?
I didn't enjoy having classes at high school right after or before my co-op position. I didn't have a car, so I'd have to run between classes to make it in time.

Overall Grade
B, for the most part enjoyable
